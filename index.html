Good to know

Frequently asked things.

What's the difference between a Home Reset and Deep Clean?

A Home Reset is one Northie for up to three hours of everyday cleaning and tidying. A Deep Clean is more detailed and covers areas not normally tackled during routine upkeep.

Do you bring cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. We arrive with the general products and equipment needed for the clean, including a vacuum. Tell us beforehand about specialist surfaces or product requirements.

Do I need to be home?

No. Access can be arranged beforehand if you're comfortable with your Northie cleaning while you're away.

Can I book regularly?

Yes. Weekly, fortnightly and other recurring arrangements can be discussed with us.

Can I request the same Northie?

You can request them and we'll do our best to maintain consistency, subject to availability.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel or reschedule at least one hour before your scheduled service. Eligible cancelled bookings are credited as a Northbound voucher for use toward a future booking. Full voucher/payment terms will be confirmed when you book.

Where do you operate?

Northbound services homes and properties across Cape Town. Send us your suburb when booking and we'll confirm availability.
